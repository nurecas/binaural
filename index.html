Two tones, one beat

left earright ear

Each ear gets its own pure tone. They differ by a few hertz, and your brain hears that difference as a slow pulse: the beat. Headphones are required, since speakers mix the two tones in the air.

With the phone locked

Press play, then lock your phone. The sound keeps going, fades in over the first 20 seconds and fades out on its own when the session ends. Pause and resume from the lock screen. Use Safari or Chrome. If you opened this page inside another app and the sound stops when you lock, open it in your browser instead.

Before you start

Turn off spatial audio or Spatialize Stereo for your headphones, so each ear hears only its own tone. Keep the volume low; the beat works at a quiet, comfortable level.

The visual

Each ring plots the left tone against the right. As their phase drifts, a circle flattens into a line and opens again. More rings per turn of the tunnel means a faster beat.

Care

Don't listen while driving. If you have epilepsy or a heart condition, check with a doctor first.
